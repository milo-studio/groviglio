Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 20, 2026

The Groviglio app was developed by Nicola Milo as a commercial service.

1. Information Collection and Use

Personal identification information (username and avatar) is stored exclusively on your device via local storage and is not transmitted to any external servers controlled by us.

2. Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob

3. Log Data and Cookies

In the event of an app error, technical data (Log Data) is collected via third-party services for debugging purposes.

4. Contact Form

The website and app include a contact form for bug reports and suggestions. Submitting the form opens your device's email client: the data provided (subject, message and email address) are transmitted to and stored in the owner's email inbox (groviglio.app@gmail.com, managed via Gmail/Google), solely for the purpose of responding to your request.

Your email address will be used exclusively to reply to your message and, only with your explicit consent via the checkbox in the form, to send you updates about the Groviglio app. You may withdraw your consent at any time by writing to groviglio.app@gmail.com. Your data will never be shared with third parties or used for any purpose other than those stated above.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our Privacy Policy, contact us at: groviglio.app@gmail.com
